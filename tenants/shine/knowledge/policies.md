# Policies

## Online membership

- **7-day free trial:** full access, no charge during the trial period. You can cancel anytime during the trial without being billed.
- **Monthly membership:** cancel anytime; cancellation takes effect at the next billing date. No partial refunds for the current month.
- **Annual membership:** pro-rated refund within 30 days of purchase; no refund after that window.

## Refunds and billing disputes

All refund requests and billing disputes are handled by a human on our team — we don't auto-process refunds via email. Reach out and we'll get back to you within two business days.

## Instructor certification refunds

Certification refunds are evaluated case-by-case. Reach out to info@shinefitness.com if you've enrolled and need to discuss.

## Class cancellations at HQ

- Drop-in: no cancellation needed; just don't come.
- Special events (workshops, retreats, intensives): refund policy specific to each event will be on the event page.

## Privacy

We don't share member data with third parties. Standard privacy practices apply — full privacy policy on shinefitness.com.
