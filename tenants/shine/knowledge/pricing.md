# Pricing & Membership

## Online membership

We offer a free 7-day trial of the full online membership — no commitment, full access to every class format. After the trial, monthly and annual membership tiers are available; current pricing is shown at signup on shinefitness.com.

The online membership includes unlimited streaming of SHiNE, SHiNE LIGHT, and UPLIFT classes (express and full-length), plus weekly fresh content.

## In-person at HQ

Drop-in and class-pack pricing for in-person classes at our Eagle, Idaho HQ are listed at shinefitness.com/dance-at-hq.

## Local certified instructors

Pricing for in-person classes with local certified SHiNE instructors is set by each instructor. Use the Location Finder at shinefitness.com/location-finder to contact instructors directly for their rates.

## Instructor certification

Become a certified SHiNE instructor and teach in your community.

- **SHiNE Certification** — $249 (regular price; sales run periodically — check shinefitness.com)
- **UPLIFT Certification** — $299 (regular price)

Certification includes training materials, choreography library access, and ongoing support from HQ.
