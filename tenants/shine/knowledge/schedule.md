# Classes & Where to Take Them

SHiNE Dance Fitness members can access classes three ways: online (anywhere in the world), at HQ in Eagle Idaho, or with a certified local instructor.

## Class formats

### SHiNE
High-cardio dance fitness combining current hits with choreography rooted in jazz, ballet, and hip-hop. HIIT format. Full-body workout including built-in toning routines. **No equipment needed.** All levels welcome.

### SHiNE LIGHT
Same fun, lower impact and complexity. Designed for anyone who wants the SHiNE experience without the higher-intensity choreography — beginners, recovering injuries, or anyone preferring a more accessible pace.

### UPLIFT
Strength training set to choreographed routines, blending iconic hits past and present. **Equipment used:** weights, bands, mats. Includes balance work and floor exercises for a well-rounded session.

## Class lengths

- Full classes: 1 hour
- Express classes: 30–45 minutes
- All formats can be modified to accommodate different fitness levels

## Where to take a class

- **Online:** new content added weekly via our online membership at shinefitness.com — free 7-day trial available
- **At HQ:** in-person classes at our Eagle, Idaho studio (370 S. Eagle Rd)
- **Local class:** use the Location Finder at shinefitness.com/location-finder to find a certified SHiNE instructor in your area
