# About SHiNE Dance Fitness

SHiNE Dance Fitness is a dance fitness brand and certification company headquartered in Eagle, Idaho. We've built a global community of instructors and members through joyful, music-driven workouts that prioritize fun, mental health, and real results — not perfection.

**Founder:** Kendall Nielson
**HQ:** 370 S. Eagle Rd, Eagle, ID 83616
**Mailing:** PO Box 2526, Eagle, ID 83616
**Brand promise:** More than fitness — community, joy, and movement that feels like a celebration, not a punishment.

## What we offer

- **Online membership** — fresh classes added weekly, three workout formats (SHiNE, SHiNE LIGHT, UPLIFT), free 7-day trial
- **Dance at HQ** — in-person classes at our Eagle, Idaho headquarters
- **Local certified instructors** — find a SHiNE instructor near you via our location finder
- **Instructor certification** — get certified to teach SHiNE, SHiNE LIGHT, or UPLIFT in your community

## Our philosophy

Workouts at SHiNE are real — not staged or rehearsed. We believe fitness should feel like a reward, not a punishment, and that mental health belongs on equal footing with physical health. The community is the heart of what we do, and decisions get made with that community in mind.
