# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you offer free estimates?**
Yes. Always free, no obligation, no pressure. We'll come walk the roof, check the attic, and give you a written report.

**How soon can you come out?**
For active leaks or storm damage, usually 2–3 business days. For routine estimates, 1–2 weeks (longer in peak storm season).

**Where do you serve?**
Asheville and a 50-mile radius — including Hendersonville, Black Mountain, Weaverville, Brevard, Marion, and Swannanoa. If you're outside that radius, we'll be honest with you about whether it makes sense.

**Do you handle insurance claims?**
Yes — we meet the adjuster on the roof, document damage thoroughly, and work directly with your insurance carrier. See `insurance_claims.md` for the full process.

**Can you give me a price over the phone or by email?**
We can give you a rough range based on what you tell us, but we don't quote firm prices without seeing the roof. Every house is different, and quoting blind is how customers get either screwed or surprise-billed. We'd rather come out for free.

**How long does a roof replacement take?**
Most asphalt shingle replacements: 1–2 days. Steeper roofs, larger homes, or metal can take 2–4 days. Weather can extend it.

**Do you do commercial roofs?**
No — residential only. We don't do flat roofs, TPO, or commercial-scale work. Happy to refer you to someone who does.

**Do you install solar?**
No, but we work with several local solar installers. If you're planning solar, we recommend doing the roof first if it's near end-of-life, then the solar install. Tearing off and reinstalling solar to replace a roof underneath later costs significantly more.

**What materials do you install?**
Architectural asphalt shingles (most common), premium asphalt, standing-seam metal, and occasionally cedar shake on historic homes.

**Do you offer financing?**
Yes — through a partner lender. Typical terms 12–84 months. Rates depend on your credit. Ask and we'll send the application link.

**Are you licensed and insured?**
Yes — NC General Contractor license #75432, general liability insurance ($2M), and full workers' comp on all employees. We can email you our certificate of insurance if you want it before we come out.

**What if I just need a small repair?**
We do those too. Most repairs are $400–$2,000 and take a single visit. We don't push replacement when a repair will do.
