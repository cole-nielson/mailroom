# Insurance Claim Process

After a major storm — hail, wind, tornado, fallen tree — your homeowner's insurance often covers roof repair or replacement. Here's how it works with us.

## Step 1: We come out and inspect (free)

If you're not sure whether you have damage, call us first. We'll walk the roof, take photos, and tell you whether it's worth filing a claim. No obligation — sometimes the answer is "you have damage but it won't meet your deductible." We'll be straight with you.

## Step 2: You file the claim

You file with your insurance — we don't do this for you. Your insurer will assign an adjuster who will schedule a visit (usually within 1–2 weeks).

## Step 3: We meet the adjuster on the roof

We strongly recommend it. Adjusters move fast and can miss things — especially:

- Soft-metal hail dings on flashing, vents, and gutters
- Wind damage to ridge cap shingles
- Hail bruising on the back side of the roof (often only visible from certain angles)

We've worked with most major insurers (State Farm, Allstate, USAA, Liberty Mutual, Erie, Nationwide, etc.) and we know what their adjusters look for. We don't charge to be there.

## Step 4: Settlement and scope

The adjuster writes a scope of work and sends a settlement to your insurer. You receive an Actual Cash Value (ACV) check first. If we agree to do the work, we'll match the insurance scope (or supplement it if anything's missing — common for things like ice-and-water shield in code).

Once the work is done, we submit final documentation and your insurer releases the depreciation (the difference between ACV and Replacement Cost Value).

## Step 5: We do the work

Same as a regular replacement. You pay your deductible to us; insurance covers the rest.

## What we won't do

- **Inflate damage.** If your roof has 8 hail strikes, we'll document 8 — not 30. Insurance fraud is a federal crime and we're not interested.
- **"Eat" your deductible.** It's illegal in NC and most states. Anyone offering to do that is committing fraud and you can be on the hook.
- **Pressure you to file.** If the damage doesn't justify a claim, we'll say so.

## Realistic timing

From storm to finished roof, plan on **4–8 weeks** for an insurance job — most of that is waiting on the adjuster and settlement. Once we have approval, the actual install is 1–2 days.
