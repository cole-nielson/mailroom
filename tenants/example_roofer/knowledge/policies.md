# Policies

## Warranty

- **Workmanship warranty:** 10 years on all full replacements. We come back and fix it if the install fails. Transferable to a new homeowner once.
- **Manufacturer warranty:** Whatever the shingle or metal manufacturer offers — typically 30–50 years. We hand you the registration paperwork at completion.
- **Repairs:** 2-year warranty on the specific repair we made.

## Scheduling

- **Storm damage:** typically out within 2–3 business days
- **Routine inspections / estimates:** typically 1–2 weeks out, longer in peak season (May–October)
- **Replacements:** scheduled within 1–6 weeks of signed contract, weather depending

We don't work in active rain or below 40°F sustained — we'll reschedule if weather doesn't cooperate.

## Cancellation

- **Before scheduling:** No charge.
- **After scheduling, before materials are ordered:** No charge.
- **After materials are ordered:** Material cost (typically 30–40% of total) plus a $250 restocking fee.
- **Day-of cancellation:** Full deposit forfeited (covers crew time and material delivery).

## Refunds

All refund requests go to a human on the team — we don't auto-process via email. Typical turnaround is 2 business days. For insurance jobs, refunds work differently and we'll walk you through it.

## Property protection

- Tarps over landscaping and AC units during tear-off
- Plywood over windows in landscape beds where shingles might fall
- Magnetic sweep of the driveway, lawn, and surrounding area before we leave
- We're insured (general liability $2M, workers' comp full coverage)

If we damage anything (we try not to, but it happens), let us know and we'll make it right.
