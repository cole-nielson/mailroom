# About Apex Roofing & Exteriors

Apex Roofing & Exteriors is a family-owned roofing contractor based in Asheville, North Carolina. Founded in 2000 by Tom Wheeler, now run alongside his son Mike, the company has spent 25 years roofing homes across western NC.

**Service area:** Asheville and surrounding 50-mile radius — Hendersonville, Black Mountain, Weaverville, Brevard, Marion, Swannanoa.

**What we focus on:** residential roofing — repairs, full replacements, and storm damage with insurance claim assistance. We don't do commercial work or solar installations.

## What makes us different

We're not the cheapest in town and we're not the biggest. What we are: the same family that's been doing this in Asheville for 25 years, with the same crews, who'll be around to honor the warranty in 10 years.

We won't try to sell you a new roof if you don't need one. We'll tell you what we'd do if it were our own house.

## Crew

We have two full crews running on busy weeks (peak storm season, late spring through fall) and one through winter. Crew leads have all been with Apex for 5+ years. We don't subcontract installs.
