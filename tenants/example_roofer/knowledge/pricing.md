# Pricing & Estimates

We don't publish exact prices because no two roofs are the same. Here's what we can tell you up front.

## Free estimates

Inspection + written estimate is always free. No obligation, no pressure, no "today only" pricing.

## Typical price ranges (residential)

These are typical for the Asheville area as of 2025. Your home may differ.

| Job | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Repair (single leak, flashing, etc.) | $400–$2,000 |
| Major repair (large area, multiple penetrations) | $2,000–$6,000 |
| Full replacement, asphalt shingle, ~2,000 sq ft single-story | $11,000–$18,000 |
| Full replacement, asphalt shingle, ~3,000 sq ft two-story or steep pitch | $17,000–$26,000 |
| Full replacement, standing-seam metal, ~2,000 sq ft | $22,000–$38,000 |
| Gutter replacement, full house | $1,800–$4,000 |
| Gutter guards (per linear ft) | $8–$15 |

What can change pricing within these ranges:

- Roof pitch (steeper costs more — labor and safety)
- Number of layers being torn off (single layer normal; tear-off of two or three adds cost)
- Decking condition (visible damage means replacement, priced per sheet)
- Material grade (impact-resistant, designer shingles, premium colors all cost more)
- Number of penetrations (skylights, chimneys, vents)
- Access (hard-to-reach roofs cost more)

## Payment

- 50% down at scheduling, balance on completion
- We accept check, credit card (3% surcharge), and ACH
- Financing available through our partner lender — typical terms 12–84 months, rates depend on credit. Ask if interested.

## Insurance jobs

If your roof is being replaced under an insurance claim, we work directly with your insurance carrier. You're typically responsible only for your deductible. We don't add costs to "make up" for your deductible — that's insurance fraud and we won't do it.
