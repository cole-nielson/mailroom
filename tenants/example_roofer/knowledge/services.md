# Services

## Roof inspections — free

We come out, walk the roof, check the attic, and give you a written report with photos. No obligation. Same-week availability for storm damage; otherwise typically 1–2 weeks out.

## Roof repairs

Leaks, missing shingles, flashing, ventilation issues, soft spots. Most repairs are completed in a single visit (1–4 hours). Pricing varies — typical range $400–$2,000 depending on scope.

## Full roof replacements

Tear-off and re-roof. We work primarily in:

- **Architectural asphalt shingles** (most common, GAF Timberline HDZ standard, 30-year warranty)
- **Premium asphalt shingles** (designer profiles, impact-resistant — common upgrade for hail-prone areas)
- **Standing-seam metal** (40+ year lifespan, ~2x cost of asphalt)
- **Cedar shake** (occasionally, mostly historic homes)

Most full replacements take 1–2 days, depending on weather and roof complexity. Includes:

- Full tear-off down to the deck
- Deck inspection and repair as needed (priced separately if significant)
- New synthetic underlayment
- Ice and water shield in valleys and around penetrations
- New shingles or metal panels
- New flashing, vents, and pipe boots
- Cleanup and magnetic sweep for nails

## Gutter work

Replacement, repair, and gutter guards. Often paired with a roof replacement; can also be done standalone.

## Storm damage and insurance claim assistance

We help homeowners through hail, wind, and tree-damage claims. We meet the insurance adjuster on site, walk the roof together, and document everything. We do not (and will not) suggest exaggerating damage — but we make sure legitimate damage doesn't get missed.

## What we don't do

- Commercial roofing (flat roofs, TPO, etc.)
- Solar installation
- Subcontracted installs — our own crews only
- Out-of-area work beyond the 50-mile service radius
